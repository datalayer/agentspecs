# Teams of applications

A team can compose [applications](../apps) as well as agents. A member names
one with `app` instead of `ref`, and is then that application: its agent, its
connections, its rules and its interface, as the application catalogue holds
them. Nothing about the application is restated in the team, so its rules
still decide every tool call and its connections are still all it reaches.

Three fields say how such a team runs:

| Field | On | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `app` | a member, the supervisor | An application of the catalogue, `id` or `id:version`. A member is an agent (`ref`) or an application (`app`), not both. |
| `runs_in` | a member | Where its loop turns: `browser`, in the person's page (the in-browser target that the Preview and `loop` use), or `runtime`, on a runtime (a cloud runtime on Datalayer, or a local one). Unsaid, wherever the team runs. |
| `talks_to` | a member | The members it asks directly while it works, each `{member, over}`. `over` is `a2a`: the member asked is served over A2A, and its agent card says what it may be asked and how it answers; or `mcp`, where the member asked is a `server` of the scene (see [Members](./members#server-string)). |
| `entry` | the team | The member a person talks to, by its id: the team's front door. Unsaid, the supervisor answers first. |

`talks_to` is not `depends_on`. `depends_on` orders work: a member waits for
another to finish. A link says who may ask whom while the work runs, in either
order and as often as needed. Both are checked when the team loads: a link or
an entry naming no member is refused, and so is a member that talks to itself.

## Sales and Accounting

`sales-and-accounting` is two applications. A person talks to Sales in their
browser. Sales asks Accounting, which runs on a runtime, for the report over
A2A, and hands over what Accounting answers. Accounting reads the Odoo books
through the `odoo-accounting` toolset of the Datalayer MCP gateway, and only
reads them.

```yaml
id: sales-and-accounting
name: Sales and Accounting
orchestration_protocol: a2a
execution_mode: supervisor
supervisor:
  name: Sales
  app: sales:0.0.1
entry: sales
agents:
  - id: sales
    app: sales:0.0.1
    role: initiator
    runs_in: browser
    talks_to:
      - member: accounting
        over: a2a
  - id: accounting
    app: accounting:0.0.1
    role: contributor
    runs_in: runtime
```

What each application brings:

- **Sales** (`apps/sales.yaml`) is a chat with no connection and no rule. Its
  instructions say it asks Accounting for every figure and never invents,
  estimates or completes one. Its assistant is the paper clip.
- **Accounting** (`apps/accounting.yaml`) connects to `odoo-accounting` at
  *Can read*, in its owner's name. Its rules read the books without asking
  and ask first before anything that would change them. Because the
  connection only reads, no tool that writes is reached at all. Its
  assistant is the wizard.

## Scenes

A *scene* — agents arranged with their roles, their shared context and
their interactions, as Datalayer's Studio shows them — is a team and nothing
else (LOOP A-04, decided 2026-10-07). Four of them are the home page's:

| Scene | Members | Under them |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `sales-and-accounting` | Sales, in the browser, asks Accounting on a runtime over A2A | Odoo, through `odoo-accounting` |
| `month-end-close` | Month-end Close alone, on a runtime | Odoo, through `odoo-accounting` |
| `crop-monitoring` | Crop Monitoring alone, on a runtime | NASA Earthdata, through `earthdata` |
| `disaster-assessment` | Event response, in the browser, asks Disaster Assessment and Change detection, each on a runtime over A2A | NASA Earthdata, under each of the two |

What the model says of a scene:

- **One member is allowed; none is not.** An agent working alone with its
  data is a scene (`month-end-close`, `crop-monitoring`: *one agent, its
  data*). A team with no member is refused when the catalogue loads.
- **Each member names its `role`** (`initiator` for the one the person
  talks to, `contributor` for the ones asked), **and the team its shared
  `context`** — how much of the conversation each member is told, and the
  Frames they all work under ([Execution](./execution#context-object)).
- **Each interaction names its protocol.** `over: a2a` to an agent or an
  application; `over: mcp` to a member that is a `server`. A member that
  talks to one not in the scene is refused, with both names in the sentence;
  so is a server asked over A2A, an application asked over MCP, a server
  that asks, and a scene that enters at a server.
- **A member runs where its credential is.** A member with a connection to a
  company's or an agency's system (`connections` on its application) turns on
  a runtime; the page talks to it over A2A. The front door of a scene that
  only asks and reports (Sales, Event response) turns in the browser.
- **Its page words** are its `name`, its `description` (one line) and its
  `suggestions`, the starters of its entry.
- **What happens on stage is the [scene spec's](../scenes)** (`scenes/`,
  `loop.scene/v1`): the setting, the script, the stage directions, the
  audience, the rehearsal and where it plays. The team stays who is on stage.

What each scene needs is said as setup, not as a mistake (`app_setup`): the
five workers behind them are catalogued and not enabled, `earthdata` is
enabled, `odoo-accounting` is not. The MCP server under a member is drawn
from its application's connections, not restated in the team.

```python
from agentspecs.teams import get_team

scene = get_team("disaster-assessment")
scene.links()               # [('event-response', 'disaster-assessment', a2a), ('event-response', 'change-detection', a2a)]
scene.referenced_apps()     # ['event-response:0.0.1', 'disaster-assessment:0.0.1', 'change-detection:0.0.1']
scene.referenced_servers()  # [] — the servers are under the applications
scene.referenced_frames()   # []
```

## From Python

```python
from agentspecs.teams import get_team

team = get_team("sales-and-accounting")
team.entry                       # 'sales'
team.referenced_apps()           # ['sales:0.0.1', 'accounting:0.0.1']
team.member("sales").talks_to    # [TeamLink(member='accounting', over=TeamProtocol.A2A)]
team.member("accounting").runs_in  # TeamPlace.RUNTIME
```

`referenced_apps()` lists every application the team names, its members and
its supervisor, so a host can check that it has them before it runs the team.
`referenced_agents()` lists the agents, as before.

## Running it

agent-runtimes serves an application of a team over A2A with fasta2a
(`loop apps run apps/accounting.yaml --cloud --a2a`), and its
`AgentA2ATeamExample` puts the two on screen as Office Assistant characters,
with the A2A exchange shown as it happens.
