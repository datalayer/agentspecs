# Stages

A Stage is something of the catalogue put on stage, and where it plays: a
[scene](../scenes) played whole, one [application](../apps), or a
[Battle](../battles). It *composes* what the catalogue already holds — there
is no second notion beside Scenes, Applications and Battles — and adds the
two things a running Stage needs that none of them says: where each member
answers on Datalayer, and whether anyone may watch without an account.

```yaml
# stages/sales-and-accounting.yaml
id: sales-and-accounting
version: 0.0.1
scene: sales-and-accounting:0.0.1     # one of scene | app | battle
deployments:
  accounting: demo-accounting         # the member on a runtime → the deployment that answers for it
public: true                          # a page of its own, no account needed
tags: [example, demo, scene]
```

Its name, its face and its description are what it stages says of itself,
unless the Stage says otherwise: the Stage above is *Sales & Accounting*,
wears 🤝 and is described as the scene is.

## Three kinds, one spec

| On stage | What plays | `deployments` |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `scene` | The scene, whole: its cast on its setting, its script. A scripted scene (`kind: scripted`) plays in the page from its lines. | Each member on a runtime, to the slug of the deployment that answers for it. None for a scripted scene. |
| `app` | One application, alone. | The application itself, to its deployment's slug. |
| `battle` | A Battle: its contestants on their ring, under its clock and referee. | None: a Battle plays on the visitor's own key. |

A Stage names exactly one of the three; two, or none, is refused as it is
read.

## Fields

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `id`, `version` | As on every spec; the file is named for the id. |
| `name`, `description`, `icon`, `emoji` | Its own when said; what it stages says otherwise. |
| `scene` | A scene of the catalogue, `id` or `id:version`, played whole. One of `scene`, `app` and `battle`. |
| `app` | An application of the catalogue, `id` or `id:version`. |
| `battle` | A Battle of the catalogue, `id` or `id:version`. |
| `deployments` | Each member to the slug of the deployment that answers for it on Datalayer, both slugs. A member must be on the scene's stage — its cast, or its team's members when it writes no cast; for an application, the application itself. Refused on a scripted scene and on a Battle. |
| `public` | Whether it has a page of its own that needs no account. `false` when unsaid. |
| `enabled`, `tags` | As on every spec. |

Every scene, application and Battle a Stage names is in the catalogue, at
the version it names when it names one; a Stage that names one the catalogue
does not hold is refused.

## Python

```python
from agentspecs.stages import get_stage, list_stages, staged_of, StageKind

stage = get_stage("sales-and-accounting")
stage.kind                 # StageKind.SCENE
stage.stages               # 'sales-and-accounting:0.0.1'
stage.deployments          # {'accounting': 'demo-accounting'}
staged_of(stage).name      # 'Sales & Accounting', the scene itself
list_stages(public=True)   # the eleven with a page of their own
list_stages(StageKind.BATTLE)   # [pong]
```
