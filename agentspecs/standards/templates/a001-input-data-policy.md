# What happens to the data people give the organization's applications

**Organization:** {{ organization }} · **Owner:** {{ owner }} · **Version:** {{ version }} · **Review by:** {{ review_on }}

## What is collected

What a person writes to an application, the documents they give it, and what it reads from the connections it holds for them.

## How it is used

- To answer the person, in that conversation.
- {{ other_uses }}
- Never to train a model, unless {{ training_exception }}.

## How long it is kept

The application's record keeps its conversations and actions for {{ retention_days }} days, then they are deleted. Documents given to it are kept until the person takes them away.

## The person's rights

A person can ask to read, take away or delete what they gave; requests go to {{ requests_contact }} and are answered within {{ request_days }} days.
